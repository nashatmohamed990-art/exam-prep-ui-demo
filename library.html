Pulmonary

Hemothorax after blunt trauma

Blood in the pleural space causes dullness and reduced breath sounds. A large loss can produce shock because the chest holds a lot of volume. This note is a study stub you can replace.

Renal

Loop diuretics and potassium

Furosemide increases sodium and potassium loss. A low potassium before the next dose is a reason to hold and call.

Biochem

Stop codons

UAA, UAG, and UGA end translation. Release factors recognize them. tRNA does not carry an amino acid for those codons.

Deferred revenue
